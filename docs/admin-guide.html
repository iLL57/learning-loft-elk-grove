Getting in

Signing in to the dashboard

The admin dashboard lives at thelearninglofteg.com/admin.html. It is a private area — visitors to the main site never see it, and there is no link to it from the public pages.

Sign in with your email and password. If you have forgotten your password, choose Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen and you will get an email with a link to set a new one. New staff get an invitation email the first time an account is created for them; the link in that email is where they set their password.

Only accounts that have been given admin access can get in. If someone who is a parent in the portal tries to open the dashboard, they are sent to the parent portal instead — the two are completely separate.

The bar across the top

  • Admin Guide — opens this document in a new tab.
  • + Invite Admin — send another staff member an invitation to create their own dashboard login. They get the same access you have.
  • Sign Out — ends your session. Worth doing on any shared or public computer.
Good to know

Every admin sees the same data and can take the same actions. There are no “view only” accounts — anyone you invite as an admin can send packages, edit records, and invite other admins.

The big picture

How a family gets enrolled

Everything in the dashboard supports one journey: a family finds the site, applies, your team reviews the application, and — if it is a fit — the family completes paperwork and payment and their child’s spot is confirmed. Once enrolled, the family can be given a login to the parent portal.

→

→
Family actsSigns the forms, pays the enrollment fee
→

→
Portal accessFamily invited to the parent portal

The dashboard’s tabs each hold one part of this: Submissions is the applications, Enrolled Students is the roster, and Families is where portal access is managed. Photo Albums, Documents, Calendar, and Field Trips & Events feed what families see once they’re in the portal. Leads & Outreach and Contact Messages are separate tools that don’t touch enrollment.

Emails to families are sent automatically at the right moments — an acknowledgement when they apply, the enrollment package when you send it, a waitlist or decline note when you choose those. They all come from enrollments@thelearninglofteg.com, and a copy of every new submission notification lands at info@thelearninglofteg.com.

Submissions

The stat cards along the top (Total, New, Package Sent, Enrolled, Waitlisted, Rejected) are counts — click one to filter the list to just those. Each row shows the date, the parent’s name and email, the children with their grades, and a status. Export CSV downloads whatever the list is currently showing, so filter first if you want a subset.

Click View on any row to open the full application and take action. A Package Sent row also shows a small progress line — for example “1 / 2 signed · 0 / 2 paid” — that updates on its own as the family completes things.

Enrolled Students

One row per child. The Documents and Initial Payment columns show Signed / Awaiting and Paid / Awaiting. These normally flip on their own when the family signs and pays, but you can click one to flip it by hand if you ever need to. The grade and payment filters narrow the list, and the row of grade counts above the table shows how full each grade is.

+ Add Student lets you enter a child directly — useful for a family enrolled outside the normal flow. Each student can be linked to a Family so the parents see them in the portal. Delete removes a student from the roster entirely.

Families

Each family is a card with contact details, the students attached to it, and a Portal Access section listing everyone who can log in for that family. This is where you + Invite to Portal, and where you send a guardian a password reset or revoke their access — see The parent portal. If a student ever shows up as not linked to a family, a banner at the top of this tab lets you attach them with one click.

Leads & Outreach

A lightweight idea board — completely separate from enrollment. Add an idea with + Add Idea, give it a channel (Instagram, a homeschool co-op, a flyer) and a status (Idea, In Progress, Completed, Paused). Nothing here sends email or touches a family.

Contact Messages

General questions sent from the site’s Contact page land here. Read one, reply from your own email, then click its status to mark it Replied. Contact messages are not applications — a family who wants to enroll still needs to fill out the application.

Photo Albums, Documents, Calendar & Field Trips

All four feed the parent portal’s Resources tiles. See Media & Resources below for how each works.

Enrolling a family

Step by step, from application to roster

This is the core workflow. Work through it from the Submissions tab.

  1. The application arrives

    When a family submits the online application it appears in Submissions as New. The family gets an automatic “we’ve received your application” email, and a notification goes to info@. Nothing else happens until you review it.

  2. Review it

    Click View to see everything the family submitted — contact details, each child and grade, how many days they want, tuition-exchange interest, whether they’re with a charter program, how they heard about you, and any message they left.

  3. Choose what happens next

    Four buttons in the application view:

    • Send Enrollment Package — the normal path. Emails the family signing and payment links right away. Continue to step 4.
    • Mark as Enrolled (No Package) — for a family that already signed and paid outside the system (in person, paper forms, etc.). Confirms grades the same way, but creates no SignWell document, no Square link, and sends no email. See the note below.
    • Add to Waitlist — emails the family that they’re on the waitlist. Waitlist offers go out oldest application first.
    • Reject — emails the family a polite “not enrolling at this time.” The application is kept, not deleted.

  4. Confirm each child’s grade

    Before the package sends (or the family is marked enrolled), you must confirm the grade level for every child, using the dropdown next to each name. It’s pre-filled with what the family entered, but whatever you confirm here becomes the child’s official enrolled grade — so correct it if needed. A grade that’s already full can’t be chosen (see Grades & capacity).

  5. The package goes out

    Sending the package does several things at once: it creates a family record and adds each child to Enrolled Students (awaiting signature and payment), and it emails the family one message containing, per child, a link to sign the enrollment forms and a link to pay the enrollment fee. The application moves to Package Sent.

  6. The family signs and pays

    Each parent opens their links, signs the forms electronically, and pays the fee. As they do, the Documents and Initial Payment markers for that child flip to Signed and Paid automatically — you don’t have to do anything. The progress line on the Submissions row keeps count. (Skipped entirely if you used Mark as Enrolled instead — the application moves straight to Enrolled.)

  7. Give the family portal access

    Once they’re enrolled, go to the Families tab, find their card, and choose + Invite to Portal. They get an email to set a password and can then log in to see their child’s status, photos, and documents. This step is optional and can be done whenever you’re ready.

Good to know

If a link in the package email isn’t ready for some reason, the family’s email tells them your team will follow up — nothing breaks, and the child still appears in Enrolled Students so you can track them.

The parent portal

What families see, and managing their access

The parent portal is at thelearninglofteg.com/portal/. It’s invite-only — a family can only log in after you’ve invited them from the Families tab. Once in, a parent sees their own family only: their children with grade, program days, and paperwork/payment status; the family’s contact details; and the shared Resources tiles — Photo Albums, Documents, Calendar, and Field Trips & Special Events are all live, with only Staff still coming.

Resources isn’t tied to having a family on file — any signed-in account sees it, which is handy for previewing the portal yourself. Only the children/family-details/guardians part of the dashboard needs an actual linked family.

Inviting a guardian

On a family’s card, + Invite to Portal opens a short form — email, name, relationship. The person gets an email to set their own password. A family can have more than one guardian; each gets their own login and they all see the same information.

Reading the access list

Each guardian on the card shows a status:

  • Invited — the invitation was sent but they haven’t signed in yet.
  • Active — they’ve signed in and set a password. The date shown is when they first logged in.

Reset and revoke

  • Send reset — emails that guardian a link to set a new password. Use it if they’re locked out.
  • Revoke — removes that guardian’s access to the family. If their login isn’t used anywhere else (not an admin, not a guardian on another family) the login itself is deleted too. A co-guardian on the same family is never affected.
Good to know

Parents can invite an additional guardian to their own family from inside the portal (up to five per family). When they do, info@ gets a heads-up email so you’re never surprised by a new login.

Media & Resources

What parents see in Resources, and how to manage it

These are the four live tiles on a parent’s portal dashboard. They work quite differently from each other — worth knowing which is which.

Photo Albums

Photos themselves stay on Google Drive or Google Photos — the dashboard just stores a link to each album. + Add Album lets you add either kind:

  • Monthly — pick a month; the label (“October 2026”) is generated automatically. Only one album per month.
  • Standing — give it its own title instead (e.g. “Student Photos”) for an ongoing album that isn’t tied to a month.

On the portal, standing albums show first in their own highlighted row, followed by monthly albums newest-first under a “Monthly Albums” heading.

Handle with care

The link has to be one anyone can open, not one that only invites specific people. In Google Drive, share the folder as “Anyone with the link.” In Google Photos, use Share → Create link — not “Invite people,” which only lets named Google accounts in. Get this wrong and parents hit a permissions wall with no error on your end to warn you.

Each album has a Visible to Parents toggle (click to flip) so you can stage a link before publishing it, and a Delete that removes the link from the portal only — it never touches the Drive/Photos album itself.

Documents

Unlike Photo Albums, these are real files uploaded straight into the site — the parent handbook, policies, forms — not links to somewhere else. + Upload Document takes a file, a title, and an optional category (e.g. “Forms”). Edit lets you rename or recategorize without re-uploading; to replace the file itself, upload it again as a new document and delete the old one.

Each document has the same Visible to Parents toggle as Photo Albums, and a Delete — but unlike a photo album link, deleting a document removes the actual file. There’s no original to fall back on.

Good to know

Documents are private by design: a parent can only open one through a one-hour link generated the moment they click, and only if it’s published. There’s no public URL for any uploaded file, unlike the Photo Albums’ “anyone with the link” model.

Calendar

Just one file — the program calendar, as a PDF. Upload Calendar PDF replaces whatever was there before; there’s no list to manage and nothing to toggle, since there’s only ever one current calendar.

On the portal page, the PDF shows right on the page (parents don’t need to download anything to read it), with Print and Open in new tab next to it.

Handle with care

Uploading a new calendar deletes the old file immediately — there’s no “previous version” to fall back on. Double-check the PDF before uploading it.

Field Trips & Special Events

A flat list for anything that needs a sign-up link or a flyer — field trips, Trunk-or-Treat, special curriculum days. + Add Item opens a form where each item is one of two types:

  • Link — for something hosted elsewhere, most often a Google Form sign-up.
  • Upload a file — for a flyer, permission slip, or anything else that’s a document, not a link.

Title is required; description and date are optional (both are just shown to parents — the date doesn’t trigger a reminder or do anything on its own). Like Photo Albums and Documents, each item has a Visible to Parents toggle and a Delete. On the portal page, items are sorted by date first (soonest first; undated items last), then newest-added.

Grades & capacity

The grade cap, and why grade confirmation matters

The program runs TK through 6th grade, with a limit of 10 students per grade. The row of grade counts above the Enrolled Students table shows how full each one is; a grade at 10 is marked FULL.

The cap is enforced whenever you send a package or mark a family enrolled. If confirming the grades in step 4 would push any grade past 10 — counting students already enrolled plus every child in that same request — it’s blocked and tells you which grade is full. A full grade also can’t be picked in the confirmation dropdown, even if it was the family’s original choice.

Handle with care

The grade you confirm is the grade the child is enrolled in — it is not just a note. If a family wrote “3rd” but the child should be in 2nd, fix it in that dropdown before confirming, not after.

Other things you can do

Beyond the main workflow

  • Add a student or family by hand — + Add Student and + Add Family for families who enrolled outside the online form. Link the student to the family so the parents see them in the portal.
  • Edit contact info — Edit on a family card updates the guardian name, email, phone, address, and emergency contacts.
  • Attach a stray student — if the Families tab shows a “not linked to a family” banner, use the dropdown there to attach the child to the right family.
  • Update payment tracking — on an Enrolled Students row you can set the enrollment date, monthly rate, next payment due, and payment status (Current, Pending, Overdue), and add notes.
  • Remove an enrolled student — Delete on their Enrolled Students row. Their original application, if any, isn’t touched.
  • Manage Photo Albums, Documents, Calendar & Field Trips — see Media & Resources.
  • Export applications — Export CSV on Submissions, respecting the current filter.
  • Invite another admin — + Invite Admin in the top bar.
  • Track outreach — the Leads tab, for your own planning.
  • Re-read this guide anytime — Admin Guide in the top bar.
Handle with care

Actions that send email, cost money, or can’t be undone easily

  • Send Package, Waitlist, and Reject email the family immediately. Each sends a real email the moment you confirm. There’s no draft or “send later.”
  • Mark as Enrolled sends nothing. It’s the opposite problem — use it only when the family genuinely doesn’t need a package, or they’ll never get their signing/payment links.
  • The confirmed grade sticks. As above — it becomes the enrolled grade.
  • Deleting an enrolled student removes them from the roster immediately. It doesn’t delete their original application.
  • Deleting a family removes its contact record and every guardian’s portal access. Its students aren’t deleted — they become unlinked and you can reattach them — but the guardians lose access and you’d need to re-invite them.
  • Revoking a guardian can delete their login (only when it’s unused elsewhere). Re-inviting later starts them fresh with a new password.
  • Deleting a document, or a Field Trip & Events item that’s a file, removes the actual file — there’s no copy kept. Deleting a photo album link, or a Field Trip & Events item that’s a link, only removes the link; nothing on the other end is touched.
  • Uploading a new calendar replaces the old one immediately — the previous PDF is deleted, not archived.
  • The manual Signed / Paid toggles are overrides. Normally these update themselves. Flip one by hand only when you know the family completed the step outside the system.
If something looks off

Quick checks

  • A family says they didn’t get an email. Ask them to check spam for a message from enrollments@thelearninglofteg.com. For a portal login, you can use Send reset on their guardian entry to send a fresh link.
  • An application isn’t showing. Check the status filter on the Submissions tab — you may be filtered to New when the row is Package Sent or Enrolled. Click Total to see everything.
  • Signed or Paid isn’t updating. It can take a few minutes after the family finishes. If it’s been longer and you’ve confirmed they completed it, flip the toggle by hand.
  • A grade won’t let you send a package or mark someone enrolled. That grade is at its 10-student limit. The family would need a different grade or a spot on the waitlist.
  • A parent says a photo album, document, calendar, or field trip link doesn’t work. Check it’s toggled Visible to Parents (not applicable to Calendar — it either has a file or it doesn’t), and — for Photo Albums or a Field Trips & Events link — that the sharing is set so anyone with the link can open it, not just specifically invited people.
  • The calendar isn’t showing on the portal. Check the Calendar tab actually has a file uploaded — until one is, the portal page just says there isn’t one yet.
  • Anything else — a button doing nothing, a screen not loading, an email that clearly went wrong — note what you did and what happened, and pass it to whoever maintains the site. Screenshots help.
